Annapurna Circuit Trek

The Annapurna Circuit Trek is a classic adventure that takes you through the stunning landscapes of the Annapurna region. This trek offers breathtaking views of the Himalayas, including iconic peaks like Annapurna I and II. Along the way, you'll experience the unique Gurung and Magar cultures, visit ancient monasteries, and witness the diverse ecosystems of the region.

Route Details

  • Duration: 14-21 days
  • Difficulty: Moderate to Hard
  • Best Season: March to May and September to November
  • Starting Point: Besisahar
  • Ending Point: Nayapul
