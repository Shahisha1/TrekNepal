Annapurna Base Camp Trek

Explore the heart of the Annapurna range and walk into the stunning natural amphitheater of the Annapurna Sanctuary.

Home > Routes > Annapurna Base Camp Trek

The Trek

The Annapurna trek begins with a ride from Pokhara to the trailhead at Nayapul. From there, the path goes up slowly through rice terraces, forests, and small mountain villages. The walk is gentle enough for beginners and gives you lots of nice mountain views.

You will pass through villages like Ghorepani and Chhomrong. Along the way, you can see mountains such as Annapurna and Machapuchare. The final part leads into the Annapurna Sanctuary, a high valley surrounded by tall snow peaks.

The trip usually takes 10-12 days. You reach Annapurna Base Camp at 4,130m. The trek mixes simple village life, fresh mountain air, and easy daily walks that many first-time trekkers enjoy.

Quick Facts

Duration 10-12 Days
Difficulty Moderate
Best Time March-May, September-November
Highest Point 4,130m

Highlights

The trek gives you wide views of mountains like Annapurna I, Annapurna South, Hiunchuli, and Machapuchare. One of the best spots is Poon Hill, where you can watch the sunrise over the mountains.

You also walk through friendly Gurung and Magar villages, cross stone bridges, and see small waterfalls. Near the end, the trail goes into a dramatic high-altitude valley with glaciers and large peaks all around.

What to Expect

Each day you walk about 4-6 hours. You stay in teahouses, which are simple lodges with beds and basic food. The weather changes as you go higher, so bring warm clothes and rain gear.

  • Mountain views: Annapurna I, Annapurna South, Hiunchuli, and Machapuchare.
  • Local culture: Gurung and Magar villages, teahouses, and warm Himalayan hospitality.
  • Altitude: The trail rises slowly with rest days for getting used to the height in places like Ghorepani and Chhomrong.
  • Terrain: You walk on dirt paths, stone steps, forest trails, and gentle alpine slopes.
