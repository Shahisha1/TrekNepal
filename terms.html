Terms & Privacy Policy

These terms explain how this demo site works and how your information is handled.

1. Terms of Use

TrekNepal is a demo website for showcasing trekking routes, travel inspiration, and account sign-up flow. By using this site, you agree to browse it for informational purposes and to interact with the available demo features.

2. Privacy Policy

This demo does not store your personal data permanently. The sign-up form is for demonstration only, and any information entered is not saved to a live database.

3. Information We Collect

  • Name and email address entered in the sign-up form
  • Optional profile details such as country, experience level, and phone number

4. Your Choices

You may choose not to submit any information, and you can leave the form at any time. If you have questions, please contact the site administrator through the contact page.
