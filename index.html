Why we built this

Nepal's trekking scene is overwhelming the first time you look into it. Permits, altitudes, seasons, porter requirements it is a lot.

This site is a plain but readable planning resource. It does not book your trip, it helps you choose the right route.

  • Filter routes by difficulty and region
  • Compare up to 3 routes side by side
  • See permit requirements upfront
  • Send an enquiry and get a reply
