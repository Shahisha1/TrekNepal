Frequently Asked Questions

Here are some common questions and answers about TrekNepal.

What is TrekNepal? +

TrekNepal is a student project from Pokhara University that provides information about trekking routes in Nepal.

How do I sign up? +

You can sign up by clicking the "Sign Up" button in the navigation bar and filling out the registration form.

Is there a mobile app? +

Currently, TrekNepal is only available as a web application. We are working on developing a mobile app in the future.

Can I contribute to the project? +

Yes! We welcome contributions from students and developers. You can contact us through the "Contact" page for more information.

Who can I contact for support? +

You can contact our support team through the "Contact" page or by emailing us at support@treknepal.com.
